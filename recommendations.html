Amble-by-the-Sea (& Coquet Estuary)

As recent arrivals & long-time visitors, we've compiled our favourite places to visit, eat, and explore. Here are our top recommendations to make the most of your stay in and around Amble-By-The-Sea.

⚓️

Amble (Coquet Estaury & Hauxley)

Amble Harbour

"The Friendliest Port" as named in the 1930s by RMS Mauretania on it's final journey, Amble sits at the mouth of the Coquet Estuary and remains a working port for trawlers & trips out to Couquet Island. Fresh catches can be purchased from Alliance Fish. The fish is seasonal so the counter changes with this in mind. Catches may consist of Langoustines, Cod, Haddock, Whiting, Monk, Halibut, Saithe, Lemon Sole, Plaice, Squid, Hake, Dover Sole, Mackerel, Herring, Red Gurnard, Red Mullet, Turbot or Brill.

📍 5-min walk ⏱️ Half-day exploring

Amble Beaches

Little Shore beach, with it's brightly coloured huts, is right in town, next to the lovely pier and harbour. With dogs you can walk south to Amble Links onward to Low Hauxley & even further toward Druridge Bay. All in the shadow of Coquet Island, home to puffins, Roseate terns, a seal colony and the mercurial dolphin pods that patrol this coastline.

📍 Amble Links 15-min walk ⏱️ Half-day walking

Amble Bord Waalk

A trail of public sculptures inspired by the bird-life found around the Amble coast. The artworks have been created by nationally and internationally renowned artists, each bringing something unique and very special to the area. To experience the virtual, poetry & sound pieces you will need to download the app which also gives additional information on the artists and a location map.

📍 The 1st scultures a 5-min walk away ⏱️ Takes as long as you need!

Amble Harbour Village

With 15 retail "pods" covering coffee & food outlets, gifts and local handicrafts, no visit to Amble is complete without a visit. You can also visit the Seafood centre & lobster hatchery. And, it's the base for Puffin Cruises.

📍 5-min walk away ⏱️ Shop as long as you want!

Coquet Island & Puffin Cruises

Coquet Island is a vital RSPB nature reserve off the Northumberland coast, famous for holding the UK's only breeding colony of Roseate Terns and home to 40,000 breeding Puffins in spring & summer every year. Puffin Cruises have a pod where you can book your trip (in advance to avoid disappointment) or even watch "puffin cam" whilst you wait!

📍 The harbour is a 5-min walk ⏱️ Each trip ~1 hour

Amble Beaches

Little Shore is right in town, next to the lovely pier and harbour. With dogs you can walk south to Amble Links anward to Low Hauxley & even further toward Drurudge Bay. In the shadow of Coquet Island.

📍 Amble Links 15-min walk ⏱️ Half-day walking

Nature & Wildlife

Hauxley Wildlife Discovery Centre

Part of the Northumberlad Wildlife Trust & one of the best wildlife watching spots in the North East (other NWT sites close by at Druridge Pools & East Chevington). At Hauxley visitors can enjoy the walks and nature trails around the reserve, the tranquillity of the hides & tasty something from The Lookout Café (try the scones!).

📍 Low Hauxley ⏱️ Free, spend as long as you like!

Seahouses Beach

Just a 5-minute walk from the cottage! Beautiful sandy beach with rock pools to explore at low tide. Perfect for dogs, families, and coastal walks.

📍 5 min walk ⏱️ Flexible

Holy Island (Lindisfarne)

A magical tidal island with an ancient priory and castle. Check tide times before visiting – you can only cross the causeway at low tide!

📍 40 min drive ⏱️ Full day
🍽️

Food & Drink

Jasper's Bistro

Join Ryan & the dedicated team in an intimate setting with the freshest of seafood cooked to order from their open kitchen. Great "Specials" & booking well in advance is essential! (We love the whisky-pairing dishes & the mountain chalet style of the window table)

📍 5 min walk 🌟 Local favourite

Sonnet

Named in the Michelin Guide & with many award wins Sonnet is a must if you love great food. They serve either an 8-course lunch or 14-course tasting menu at dinner; focused on the best quality ingredients, with the emphasis being on 'simplicity'. (We prefer to visit at lunch).

📍 Alnwick, 20 min drive 🌟 Award-winning!

Fish Shack

Part of the Old Boat House group & situated on Amble's quayside, Fish Shack is a cosy, rustic eatery that was purpose built using the old 'Sea Quest' boat & boasts great views too! Their sister restaurant is near the pods.

📍 10-min walk 🌟 Must-try

Practical Information

Nearest Supermarkets

Amble has a Tesco for essentials & very close to the cottage a larger Morrisons supermarket. For more options, Alnwick (20 min) has Turnbulls and Aldi, or Morpeth (30 min) has a wider selection.

📍 Various

Vets

The nearest vet is a few minutes walk or drive from the cottage. We can provide contact details and directions on request – just ask when you book!

Most beaches are dog-friendly year-round. Check tide times before heading to Holy Island. Lifeguards patrol some beaches in summer months.

Coastline & Castles

Northumberland boasts more castles than any other English county, set against a dramatic coastline designated an Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty.

Warkworth Castle

Magnificent medieval fortress overlooking the Coquet

Bamburgh Castle

Iconic fortress rising above golden sands

Dunstanburgh Castle

Dramatic ruins reached only on foot across clifftops

Alnwick Castle

The real Hogwarts - Harry Potter filming location

Holy Island

Lindisfarne - tidal island with ancient priory

Seahouses

Gateway to the Farne Islands and seal watching

Craster

Famous for kippers and stunning coastal walks

Farne Islands

Seabird sanctuary with seals and St Cuthbert's chapel

Cheviots & The Interior

Venture inland to discover rolling hills, ancient forests, and the wild beauty of Northumberland National Park and the Dark Sky Reserve.

Hadrian's Wall

UNESCO World Heritage Roman frontier. Discover the remains of the forts & towns that once kept watch over the 73-mile long wall at variuos locations

Kielder Water & Forest

Home to England’s largest forest and the biggest man-made lake in Northern Europe

Northumberland International Dark Sky Park

At 580sq miles, Europe's second largest Dark Sky Reserve. Open year round
