Our Recommendations

Explore Northumberland

As locals, we've compiled our favourite places to visit, eat, and explore. Here are our top recommendations to make the most of your stay.

🏰

Castles & Heritage

Bamburgh Castle

One of England's most iconic fortresses, dramatically perched on a volcanic outcrop overlooking the sea. Explore the stunning state rooms and learn about over 1,400 years of history.

📍 5 min drive ⏱️ Half day

Dunstanburgh Castle

A dramatic 14th-century castle ruin accessible only by foot. The scenic coastal walk from Craster is one of Northumberland's finest, with stunning views throughout.

📍 15 min drive + walk ⏱️ Half day

Alnwick Castle & Gardens

Known as the 'Windsor of the North' and famous as a Harry Potter filming location. Don't miss the spectacular Alnwick Garden with its unique water features.

📍 20 min drive ⏱️ Full day
🐦

Nature & Wildlife

Farne Islands

Home to thousands of puffins, grey seals, and seabirds. Boat trips depart from Seahouses harbour – an absolute must-do! Best visited April-July for puffin season.

📍 Seahouses harbour ⏱️ 2-4 hours

Seahouses Beach

Just a 5-minute walk from the cottage! Beautiful sandy beach with rock pools to explore at low tide. Perfect for dogs, families, and coastal walks.

📍 5 min walk ⏱️ Flexible

Holy Island (Lindisfarne)

A tidal island with an ancient priory and castle. Check the tide times carefully! The causeway is only accessible at low tide, making the visit feel like a true adventure.

📍 25 min drive ⏱️ Half day
🍽️

Food & Drink

The Good Earth

Excellent local shop very close to the cottage selling organic produce, artisan bread, local cheeses, and everything you need for a gourmet stay.

📍 2 min walk 🌟 Local favourite

Seahouses Fish & Chips

You can't visit the coast without proper fish and chips! Several excellent chippies in Seahouses – nothing beats eating them overlooking the harbour.

📍 10 min walk 🌟 Traditional favourite

Craster Kippers

Visit the famous L. Robson & Sons smokehouse in Craster to see traditional kipper smoking and pick up some of the finest smoked fish in Britain.

📍 15 min drive 🌟 Must-try
🚶

Activities

Coastal Walking

Miles of stunning coastal paths right on your doorstep. Walk from Seahouses to Bamburgh along the beach for spectacular castle views, or try the Dunstanburgh route.

📍 From cottage 🐾 Dog friendly

Cycling

Quiet country lanes and dedicated cycle routes make this area perfect for exploring on two wheels. Bike hire available locally.

📍 Various routes ⏱️ Flexible

Water Sports

Kayaking, paddleboarding, and surfing are all popular along this stretch of coast. Several local providers offer lessons and equipment hire.

📍 Local beaches ⏱️ Half day
ℹ️

Practical Information

Nearest Supermarkets

Amble has a Tesco for essentials & very close to the cottage a larger Morrisons supermarket. For more options, Alnwick (20 min) has Turnbulls and Aldi, or Morpeth (30 min) has a wider selection.

📍 Various

Vets

The nearest vet is a few minutes walk or dive from the cottage. We can provide contact details and directions on request – just ask when you book!

📍 Amble-By-The-Sea

Beach Safety

Most beaches are dog-friendly year-round. Check tide times before heading to Holy Island. Lifeguards patrol some beaches in summer months.

🐾 Dog friendly
