Welcome

Escape to the Northumberland Coast

Nestled in Northumberland's Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, Puffling Cottage offers the perfect base for exploring one of England's most breathtaking coastlines.

Castle Views

Puffling Cottage is just a short walk along the River Coquet from the iconic Warkworth Castle & Hermitage, the home from 14th century onwards of the powerful Percy family. Northumberland, with around 70 castles, has the most of any county in England. The ever popular and spectacular Alnwick, Dunstanburgh & Bamburgh castles are also within very easy reach.

Northumberland Beaches

Access over 40 miles of golden wide sandy beaches & dune systems, consistently rated among the finest in the UK, perfect for walks and relaxation. Puffling Cottage is within walking distance of Little Shore next to Amble Harbour and Amble Links on the edge of town.

Dog Friendly

Your four-legged family members are welcome! The cottage is fully equipped for dogs with beds, bowls, treats and a secure garden.

Puffin Paradise

Visit Coquet Island (See Dave, Steven & the team at Puffin Cruises), home to puffins and many other seabirds (incl. the only nesting site for Roseate Terns), seals & possibly dolphins too - right on our doorstep!

Food Heaven

From award-winning food from Sonnet in Alnwick to the lovely Jaspers a short stroll from Puffling Cottage, Northumberland offers a food paradise. In Amble you can buy fish fresh from the boat at Alliance Fish, visit many independent eateries (Fish Shack, The Old Boathouse etc) and there is the Harbour Fish Bar for great fish & chips!

Activity Variety

Whether you prefer coastal walks, cycling, sailing, kayaking, surfing or other water sports, or simply relaxing with a good book, there's something for everyone. Golf courses, nature reserves, and historic sites are all within easy reach of Puffling Cottage.
