Discover Northumberland

From ancient castles to wildlife-rich islands, there's something for everyone on this magnificent coast.

🏰

Bamburgh Castle

Explore over 1,400 years of history at this iconic fortress perched dramatically above the beach.

🐧

Farne Islands

Take a boat trip to see puffins, seals, and other incredible wildlife on these protected islands.

⛪

Holy Island

Visit the mystical Lindisfarne, accessible by causeway, with its ancient priory and castle.

🌟

Dark Skies

One of Europe's largest Dark Sky Parks offers exceptional stargazing opportunities.
