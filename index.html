Welcome

Escape to the Northumberland Coast

🐧

Nestled in the heart of Northumberland's Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, Puffling Cottage offers the perfect base for exploring one of England's most breathtaking coastlines.

🏰

Castle Views

Just minutes from the iconic Bamburgh Castle, one of England's most spectacular fortresses with over 1,400 years of history.

🏖️

Pristine Beaches

Access miles of golden sandy beaches, consistently rated among the finest in the UK, perfect for walks and relaxation.

🐾

Dog Friendly

Your four-legged family members are welcome! The cottage is fully equipped for dogs with beds, bowls, and a secure garden.

🐦

Puffin Paradise

Visit the Farne Islands, home to thousands of puffins, seals, and seabirds – a wildlife experience like no other.
